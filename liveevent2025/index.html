January

Hunan TV Countdown Party

January 1
Venue: Haikou - Wuyuan River Stadium

Hua Chenyu participated in Hunan TV’s countdown party for 12 consecutive years.
This year, Hua performed two new songs, “Bizarre Psychology AKA Quirky Psychology (怪诞心理学)“ and “In the Morning Light, There’s You (晨光你有你)“, which were first released at the “Mars Concert 2024“.
In addition, Hua and Idol group TNT (时代少年团) together performed the song “我要你管”, a mix of their respective masterpieces “I Don’t Care (我管你)“ and “要你管”.

April

Beloong Music Festival, Wuhang

April 20
Venue: Wuhan - INTIME Music Park

After six years, on April 20, Hua Chenyu returned to the music festival stage! With his full concert band lineup and brand-new VJ animations specially created for the festival. Hua delivered six songs to his hometown audience in Wuhan. The setlist included fan-dubbed “commercial performance package” like “I Don’t Care (我管你)“ and “For Forever“, alongside recent releases “Black and White Artist“, “Bizarre Psychology AKA Quirky Psychology“, “Extraordinary Ordinary Life“, and “Never Cross Paths AKA Eternal Farewell “, culminating with the classic Martian love ballad “King and Beggar“.
Notably, “Black and White Artist“ marked its debut performance outside Hua’s solo concerts.

  • “Black and White Artist (黑白艺术家)“

May

Changchun Aura Music Festival

May 25
Venue: Changchun - Olympic Park

On May 25, Hua Chenyu performed at the Aura Music Festival in Changchun. This marked his first live performance in China’s northeastern region and served as a warm-up for his upcoming Mars Concert in the northeastern city of Shenyang next month.
After last weekend’s Mars Concert in Qingdao, Hua caught a cold, so he performed at this festival while under the weather. He could be seen occasionally covering his microphone to cough quietly, but he still maintained a high vocal standard, delivering a total of 8 songs – one more than at last month’s music festival in Wuhan!

July

Zhangbei Grassland Music Festival

July 26
Venue: Zhangjiakou - Zhangbei Zhongdu Original Grassland

On July 26, Hua Chenyu visited Hebei Province, to participate in the Zhangbei Grassland Music Festival. On Zhangbei Grassland, rare consecutive heavy rains hit the drought-stricken grassland, turning the lawn into a pond deeper than the calf. The environment was extremely harsh.
Equipment was damaged by the rain, making rehearsals impossible. After taking the stage, Hua and the band members found their in-ear monitors silent – only the metronome worked. So they sang three songs along with only the metronome. The accompaniment that followed was also intermittent, but it did not affect his performance at all.
Braving the rain, Hua Chenyu stated that he took the stage in his casual clothes, as fans had wished during the live stream. Coincidentally, this entire outfit – from head to toe – was waterproof.
The setlist included “Searching for Myself (好想我回来啊)“, a song that had never been performed at a festival before.

  • “I Don’t Care (我管你)“

  • “Black and White Artist (黑白艺术家)“

  • “Extraordinary Ordinary Life (普通到不普通的人生)“

  • “Searching for Myself (好想我回来啊)“

  • “Never Cross Paths AKA Eternal Farewell (不重逢)“

  • “Light Up the Bonfire at the Galaxy’s End (点燃银河尽头的篝火)“

  • “King and Beggar (国王与乞丐)“

PEACE NIGHT of “Game For Peace”

August 9
Venue: Sanya - Egret Stadium, Sanya Sports Center

On August 9, Hua Chenyu participated as a celebrity endorsement in the “PEACE NIGHT” of the mobile game “Game For Peace”.
All four celebrity endorsements of “Game For Peace” (Hua Chenyu, Victoria Song, Tian Xiwei, Esther Yu) appeared in cosplay. Hua cosplayed the character “Nova Fierce Loong (新星烈龙)” (wearing the headpiece of “Fantasy Rocket (梦幻火箭)”).
In addition to the 5th Anniversary Theme Song “Eternal Flame AKA The onquenchable fire (永不熄灭的火焰)“, Hua also performed his original song “Bizarre Psychology AKA Quirky Psychology (怪诞心理学)“.
In the exhibition match, Hua led his team to victory.

Tencent Video JUMPPARK Music Festival

August 16
Venue: Qingdao - the tarmac of the closed former Liuting Airport

On August 16, Tencent Video JUMPPARK Music Festival was held at the tarmac of Qingdao’s former Liuting Airport. Hua Chenyu closed the event with 8 of his representative songs.
Hua Chenyu appeared with silvery-white long hair, paired with avant-garde stage costumes. He took the stage on a suspended extended platform to perform, creating a mysterious atmosphere where “the voice can be heard but the person cannot be seen”, and the footage shot from an overhead angle stunned the entire audience.
Hua redesigned the opening music for this music festival. The stage design integrated laser effects, drone performances, and spraying of ribbon paper, and also adopted the visual logo of his Mars Concert, thus creating an immersive “mini Mars Concert” experience. Hua Chenyu asked the fans on site, “Did you see the surprise?”

  • “Illusion and Reality (虚幻与现实)“

  • “I Don’t Care (我管你)“

  • “Black and White Artist (黑白艺术家)“

  • “Never Cross Paths AKA Eternal Farewell (不重逢)“

  • “Searching for Myself (好想我回来啊)“

  • “King and Beggar (国王与乞丐)“

  • “Light Up the Bonfire at the Galaxy’s End (点燃银河尽头的篝火)“

September

“Follow the Singers to Huaihua” Concert

September 20
Venue: Huaihua Sports Center Main Stadium

On September 20, 2025, the “Follow the Singers to Huaihua” Concert, part of the 5th Tourism Development Conference of Hunan Province, was held in Huaihua City. The entire concert was live-streamed on Mango TV and aired on Hunan Satellite TV the next day.
The concert pioneered an interactive segment called “Travel Blind Box”. Hua Chenyu opened the “Gourmet Emoji Blind Box” and showcased a variety of Huaihua’s specialty dishes through “audio scenes + visual symbols”. The on-site audience guessed the dish names while listening to the music, and those who guessed correctly could redeem a physical blind box.
The stage design for “Bizarre Psychology AKA Quirky Psychology (怪诞心理学)“ was extremely bizarre. A group of backup dancers each held up huge portraits of Hua and danced around him. During the final chorus, the dancers even lifted Hua horizontally above their shoulders, allowing him to lie on top of the crowd and sing.

  • “Bizarre Psychology AKA Quirky Psychology (怪诞心理学)“

  • “Light Up the Bonfire at the Galaxy’s End (点燃银河尽头的篝火)“

  • “Beauty Yet Revealed to Me (那些我尚未知道的美丽)“

Venue: Foshan - Foshan Grassland Music Venue

On September 21, 2025, Hua Chenyu returned to Foshan to perform at the Bubbling&Boiling Music&Arts Festival, following his solo concert during the May Day holiday.
Hua Chenyu took the stage wearing a simple striped T-shirt and jeans. The T-shirt was very similar to the one he wore at the Shanghai Zebra Music Festival in 2018, and the jeans have also been a favorite item that has frequently appeared in private settings such as rehearsal photos since 2018. This outfit evoked memories for the fans.
Additionally, this was also the first time Hua performed “Eternal Flame (永不熄灭的火焰)“ at a music festival.

November 10
Venue: Nanjing - Gucheng Lake Water Slow City

A music festival hosted by Weibo, China’s largest social media platform.
Hua Chenyu showed off his platinum-blonde long hair again, pairing an all-black outfit with black leather gloves and black sunglasses. Holding a white microphone, he exuded intense visual tension in the rain.
This music festival was a schedule that had been announced early at Hua Chenyu’s Nanjing Concerts during the weeklong National Day holiday. At the music festival, a promotional video for his upcoming Mars Concerts to be held at the Wenchang International Aerospace City at the end of this month was also played.

Oxygen Music Festival

November 15
Venue: Hangzhou - Grand Canal Hanggang Park

Hua Chenyu took the stage with his natural hair for the first time in a long while. His fluffy curly hair adorned with a few simple hair clips created an adorable look that drew constant screams from the audience.
The temperature was low that day. Hua Chenyu wore a windproof jacket with contrasting colors of red, yellow, and blue, occasionally pulling up the collar or tucking his hands into the opposite sleeves. However, the powerful explosive energy of his songs quickly ignited the atmosphere at the venue.
