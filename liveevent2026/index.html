August

PSI Grand Finals & PEACE NIGHT of “Game For Peace”

August 8
Venue: Shenzhen - Shenzhen Sports Center Stadium

On August 8, Hua Chenyu participated as a celebrity endorsement in the PSI Grand Finals & PEACE NIGHT of the mobile game “Game For Peace”, with the costume of the classic in‑game skin “Whimsical Rabbit (童趣迷兔)”.
Hua performed the 7th Anniversary Theme Song “Forever brave and young (千万次奔向)“ as well as his original song “Light Up the Bonfire at the Galaxy’s End (点燃银河尽头的篝火)“. He later teamed up with pro players for an exhibition match.

  • “Light Up the Bonfire at the Galaxy’s End (点燃银河尽头的篝火)“

  • “Forever brave and young (千万次奔向)“

April

“Game For Peace” Peace Elite League (PEL) Finals

April 26
Venue: Changsha - Hunan Radio and Television Program Production Base

On April 26, 2026, at the final match of PEL 2026 Spring Season, Hua Chenyu attended as the celebrity endorsement and delivered the first offline performance of PEL’s new theme song, “Forever brave and young (千万次奔向)“.
Hua Chenyu first kicked off the finals with a fiery performance of “Eternal Flame (永不熄灭的火焰)“. Later, during the halftime show, he performed “Iconic moment (名场面)“ and”Forever brave and young (千万次奔向)“ while cosplaying the “Vow of Time (时光之誓)” outfit paired with @花傲天 ‘s signature hairstyle.

  • “Eternal Flame (永不熄灭的火焰)“

  • “Iconic moment (名场面)“

  • “Forever brave and young (千万次奔向)“



























China Blue Zuili Lake Music Festival

April 25
Venue: Jiaxing - Zuili Lake Central Park

A music festival hosted by Zhejiang STV. Due to its logo’s color scheme, Zhejiang STV is nicknamed “The Blue Station,” which is why the festival is branded as “China Blue”. During the interactive segment following the performances, the host, representing Zhejiang STV, expressed their anticipation and extended an invitation to Hua Chenyu for more performance collaborations in the future.
Just a few days prior, the MARS CONCERT 2026 scheduled to be the opening stop of “Mars Park-Style Concerts 2.0” at Fuxian Lake in Yuxi, Yunnan, during China’s May Day holiday, were abruptly cancelled due to force majeure. In the interactive segment, fans played a surprise video on the giant screen and sang “In the Morning Light, There’s You“ in unison to comfort Hua Chenyu. Hua, in turn, comforted the audience, sharing that on the very night the cancellation was announced, he had already organized a specialized task force to scout other Chinese cities for a suitable new venue.

  • “Illusion and Reality (虚幻与现实)“

  • “I Don’t Care (我管你)“

  • “Eternal Flame (永不熄灭的火焰)“

  • “Black and White Artist (黑白艺术家)“

  • “Never Cross Paths AKA Eternal Farewell (不重逢)“

  • “For Forever“

  • “King and Beggar (国王与乞丐)“

2026 Hunan TV Lantern Festival Gala

March 3
Venue: Huguan, Shanxi - Happy Taihang Valley

Hua Chenyu performed as the grand finale guest at the 2026 Hunan TV Lantern Festival Gala.
Due to the freezing weather at the live broadcast site – with heavy snow even falling during rehearsals a few days prior – visible frost could be seen with every breath Hua took while singing. During the previous day’s rehearsal, Hua wore an exceptionally puffy bright yellow down jacket, which earned him the affectionate nickname “Honey Butter Bun” from the fans.

  • “Let’s Go and Watch the Sunrise (走，一起去看日出吧)“

  • “Beauty Yet Revealed to Me (那些我尚未知道的美丽)“

January

Hunan TV Countdown Party

January 1
Venue: Haikou - Wuyuan River Stadium

Hua Chenyu participated in Hunan TV’s countdown party for 13 consecutive years.
This year, Hua first performed a solo medley of three of his signature tracks: “Great Sage Equal To Heaven“, “King and Beggar“ and “Growing Toward the Sun - Sunrise Ver.“. Among these, “Growing Toward the Sun - Sunrise Ver.“ featured middle school students in uniforms as backup dancers. The choreography, which they created themselves, brimmed with boundless vitality and energy, sparking a huge buzz on short-video platforms.
Next, Hua led the idol group TNT (时代少年团) in a float parade across the venue in the moments right before the countdown.
Then, after the countdown to ring in the New Year, Hua performed his signature track “I Don’t Care“ alongside the six finalists from “Sound Trek (声鸣远扬) 2025”, the music audition program where he serves as a mentor.

  • “Great Sage Equal To Heaven (齐天大圣)“

  • “King and Beggar (国王与乞丐)“

  • “Growing Toward the Sun - Sunrise Ver. (向阳而生日出版)“

  • The float parade with TNT

  • “I Don’t Care (我管你)“
