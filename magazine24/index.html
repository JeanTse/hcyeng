February

“China Daily”

Exclusive Interview: “Hua Chenyu: Music is Discovery, Not Creation“

On the eve before his Shenzhen concerts, Hua Chenyuyu sat down for an exclusive interview with China Daily’s flagship column “The Sound of Excellence”. “I want to create a ‘Utopian’ space where everyone can feel as relaxed as they do at home,” he shared.

“China Daily” is an English daily newspaper affiliated with the Propaganda Department of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China. Its main readers are diplomats, foreigners, tourists and Chinese people who want to improve their English. It has the widest print circulation of any English-language newspaper in China.
