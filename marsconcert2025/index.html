1. Achievements

  • This year, Hua Chenyu has completely updated the setlist and arrangements. The arrangement theme is “East-West Fusion,” skillfully blending traditional Chinese instruments such as guzheng, xiao, and pipa with Western instruments like harp, Scottish bagpipes, and Irish flutes. Through the collision of Eastern and Western musical elements and cross-cultural art fusion, he has expanded his musical boundaries anew.

  • May 4 is the first anniversary of the sunrise concert. 2,000 drones were launched to simulate the sun and spell out the words: “You are my sun — by Big Brother. (你们就是我的太阳 ——大哥)”, setting a record for the number of drones launched above the concert venue. (Due to the large gathering of people at the performance site, for safety reasons, the launch of such a large number of drones is usually not allowed.)

  • During the Foshan concerts, the fans ate a total of 3 tons of rice in three days (“Chenyu Fried Rice” was distributed for free, each fan could line up multiple times to receive a 50ml cup-sized serving).

  • On February 7, 2026, at the concert venue celebrating Hua Chenyu’s 36th birthday, the audience spontaneously organized across various viewing sections to create a spectacular giant mosaic. By raising different colored fluorescent boards, they formed massive characters that illuminated the night.

2. Anecdote

  • Hua Chenyu opened the performance by singing the Baroque rock-style song “Illusion and Reality“ while descending from mid-air on a spiral-shaped lift stage fully decorated with diamonds.

  • The hit song “Ashes from Fireworks“ from Hua’s debut album “Quasimodo’s Gift“ was composed by indie musician Xilou. Hua Chenyu stated during his cover of Xilou’s representative work “Child“ on “Singer 2018“ that “Ashes from Fireworks“ could be seen as a prelude to “Child“. He masterfully fused the harmonies of both songs, finally presenting them in mashup format this time.

  • Similarly, Hua mashed up two songs - “Extraordinary Ordinary Life“ and “Beauty Yet Revealed to Me“. Originally twin tracks sharing the same melody with different lyrical themes and arrangements, they were remixed under the new title “From Ordinary to Beauty Yet Revealed” and incorporated into the medley segment.

  • On the second day of the Foshan concerts, there was a heavy rain. But the heavy rain didn’t dampen the fans’ enthusiasm – many of them started playing in the rain, which eased Hua’s worries about everyone.

  • Also, during the Foshan concerts, the fans ate a total of 3 tons of rice in three days (“Chenyu Fried Rice” was distributed for free, each fan could line up multiple times to receive a 50ml cup-sized serving).

  • Hua Chenyu engaged in “weather-based bet” with fans during concerts in various cities. This was a plan inspired by his “Little Sun” legend (believed to bring sunshine to concert dates). It was decided that if it rained, Hua would win, and if it was sunny, the fans would win, with both sides experiencing victories and defeats. As punishment, Hua danced to the choreography from the “Bizarre Psychology AKA
    Quirky Psychology    “ MV in Xi’an. Later, in Qingdao, he proposed and fulfilled a reward of enjoying the sight of fans singing from the audience while eating and drinking on stage.

  • The concert held on February 7th (Hua’s 36th birthday), marked the first public birthday celebration Hua has held since his debut.

  • A different sentence was printed each day on the long ribbon paper that was sprayed at the end of the performance.

Fuzhou

  • On March 8-9, Hua Chenyu launched his annual tour with two consecutive concerts in Fuzhou, the capital of Fujian Province in southern China.

  • The March 8 premiere coincided with International Women’s Day. Hua Chenyu sent special blessings to attending fans by SMS: “As spring blossoms, Fuzhou extends its invitation. On the occasion of International Women’s Day, may each unique soul among you become the wind, the ocean, and an eternal flame, forever growing towards the sun! - Hua Chenyu 2025.3.8”

  • During the second day’s performance, immediately after singing “Arrival“, the lift stage malfunctioned. With parkour-like agility, Hua leapt down multiple platform levels consecutively, igniting frenzied cheers throughout the venue.

Changsha

  • On March 22-23, Hua Chenyu returned to his debut city Changsha to hold Concerts for two consecutive days.

  • A drone conducting high-speed aerial filming around and the stage crashed at a corner of the platform, with the entire incident broadcast live on the giant screen in first-person perspective.

  • While singing the line “There is a letter, A letter for you from the future” in “Let’s Go and Watch the Sunrise“, Hua pulled out a letter from his chest. The stationery featured a simple sketch widely cherished among the fans: Hua and ET sharing a kiss at sunrise.

  • During the second day’s concert, Hua made a weather-based bet with fans, rooted in his “Little Sun” legend (believed to bring sunshine to concert dates). Despite prior continuous rain, Changsha experienced unusually scorching weather during the concerts, followed by a sudden temperature drop the next day. Hua pledged that If the next concert in Xi’an also has clear skies, fans win—and he’ll grant one collective wish.

Xi’an

  • April 5-6 (Qingming Festival holiday in China), Hua Chenyu held consecutive concerts in Xi’an, an ancient capital of 13 dynasties.

  • On Day 1, Hua mentioned “keeping the performance more family-friendly” as his father and sister were in the audience, while teasing a “an outfit requiring prolonged mental preparation” for Day 2. Fans speculated wildly about its boldness, only to discover a full-body leopard print ensemble the next day.

  • The most renowned historical site in Xi’an is the Mausoleum of the First Qin Emperor’s Terracotta Army. Staffs from a Terracotta Warriors-themed musical troupe watched the lives in Qin Shi Huang and warrior cosplay. During the performance of “Light Up the Bonfire at the Galaxy’s End“ they joined fans’ dancing in the arena pit.

  • While filming a vlog at a snack street the day before, Hua passed by a fan girl without being recognized. At the concert, Hua publicly screened the coincidentally recorded encounter, jokingly awarding her an autographed “reunions feel distant” (from “In the Morning Light, There’s You“ lyrics) certificate, and gifted her a whole set of merchandise.

  • Having lost the weather-based bet with fans (no rain occurred), Hua voluntarily proposed fulfilling the wager by dancing to choreography from his “Bizarre Psychology AKA Quirky Psychology“ MV - his first official concert dance performance. On Day 2, Hua executed the routine wearing Lop-eared rabbit shaped woolen hat, then turned completely red from shyness.

Foshan

  • China celebrated Labor Day from May 1 to 5, and Hua Chenyu held three consecutive Theme Park-style Concert from May 2 to 4 in Foshan. The third day (May 4) marked the first anniversary of the sunrise concert, and Hua prepared many surprises for the Martians.

  • Hua Chenyu sent gift boxes by express delivery to audiences in the inner area (CN¥1280/980). The boxes included a small bottle of perfume, lipstick, a key-shaped ornament, and a plastic wristband.

  • Hua Chenyu has twice posted photos on social media of himself cooking fried rice. Encouraged by enthusiastic Martians, he promised to treat everyone to his “Chenyu Fried Rice.” He shared his fried rice recipe and set up “Chenyu Fried Rice” booths, offering free portions to all (each person could line up multiple times to receive a 50ml cup-sized serving). At the concert, he mentioned that a total of 3 tons of rice were consumed over the three days.

  • During the live broadcast on the day before the performance, Hua Chenyu fried rice at the stall on the spot. He originally wanted to distribute the fried rice to the fans who were watching outside the venue, but due to the large safety distance on both sides of the enclosure, he couldn’t hand it out and had to give up with regret.












  • Various facilities in the concert were upgraded, including:

    • Air-conditioned rest area in the VIP section (The large tent was enclosed with transparent plastic, allowing people to rest while still being able to watch the concert at the same time.)
    • All areas provided free drinking water, ice, and anti-heatstroke medicine.
    • Several high-end mobile toilets and changing rooms were set up, with an increased number of women’s restrooms, along with a variety of feminine products.
    • After a brief heavy rain on the second day, when the lawn became muddy, buckets and water hoses were provided for washing feet.

  • In the afternoon sessions of the concert, the usual game segment was replaced with an Airplane Chess featuring various obstacles, intentionally designed to make Hua receive “punishments.” He ended up doing many things he normally wouldn’t do, such as dancing, mimicking emojis, taking rapid selfies, and creating live drawing.



  • In keeping with local traditions, a lion dance troupe was invited to perform in the plaza on the third day.

  • On the first day of the concert, the weather was hot. On the second day, there was a brief heavy rain during the middle of the event. The game segment was skipped, and many pieces of equipment were damaged. Repairs caused a 20-minute delay to the evening session. During the rain, audiences were allowed to leave the venue to take a break, and re-entry was arranged once the rain had stopped.

  • The heavy rain didn’t dampen the fans’ enthusiasm – many of them started playing in the rain, which eased Hua’s worries about everyone. During the evening session, he mentioned that he had seen various videos online of fans having fun in the water.

  • On the third day, to celebrate the first anniversary of the sunrise concert, Hua had a custom cake made (see photo).
    Zuo Li prepared a custom banner (see photo), with the words: “This is a remarkable first anniversary of the sunrise concert!”


  • The surprise of the evening show on the third day was that 2,000 drones were launched to simulate the sun and spell out the words: “You are my sun — by Big Brother. (你们就是我的太阳 ——大哥)”. It had been kept secret from the fans all the time. The drones could only be flown when the weather was fine at night. So, since the daytime, Hua had been particularly worried. He checked the sky several times and talked about various topics related to the sky.




  • On the third day, just like during last year’s sunrise concert, over ten thousand fans who couldn’t get tickets gathered outside the venue to watch. Hua Chenyu greeted them through the big screen. During the “kiss” segment, he even asked the cameraman to come onstage and zoom in close, so that his face would appear more clearly on the big screen, making it easier for those watching from far outside the venue to see.

Qingdao

  • On May 17, Hua Chenyu continued his live tour in Qingdao.

  • Due to rain on the second day of the Foshan concerts, Hua won this “weather-based bet”. He proposed a fun trade-off for the Qingdao concert: he wanted to experience the same pleasure fans have—relaxing, eating, and even lying down while watching a concert! Consequently, at the following Qingdao concert, the fans performed a full sing-along of “Light Up the Bonfire at the Galaxy’s End“. This allowed Hua to relax on stage, enjoying local barbecue and beer. He even shared a toast with his band and downed a full glass of beer on the spot.

Chengdu

  • From May 31 to June 1, coinciding with China’s traditional Dragon Boat Festival and International Children’s Day respectively, Hua Chenyu held two consecutive concerts in Chengdu.

  • This year, the VJ for “The World is a Zoo“ features real-time rendering animation that randomly transforms the singer and audience into cartoon animal images, which is quite intriguing. For the Chengdu concerts, it was a panda-themed special edition, where everyone’s transformed image became a panda, and the background turned into a bamboo forest habitat scene.

  • During the live stream the night before the concert, Hua accepted fans’ suggestions and challenged a high ponytail hairstyle for the second day’s performance. Additionally, since Hua announced the high ponytail during the first day’s concert, fans flocked to take overnight flights to attend! Tickets sold out completely that same day. During the performance, Hua, who noticed fans’ overnight travel updates on social media, kindly reminded everyone to rest and eat properly.

  • Also during the live stream, Hua agreed to bring a camera on stage when the opportunity arises, allowing everyone to see his perspective from the stage. True to his word, after the performance, Hua released a first-person selfie video specially recorded during rehearsals, showing his entrance descending onto the stage from above.

  • The second day of the performance coincided with International Children’s Day, and Hua invited visually impaired children to the concert through a charity organization.

Zhengzhou

  • On June 14, Hua Chenyu continued his live tour in Zhengzhou, Henan Province.

  • Due to rain on the first day of the Chengdu concert, Hua won the “weather-based bet” again. His prize request was an a cappella chorus performed by the entire audience. He divided the tens of thousands of fans into three vocal sections based on their seating areas and taught each group a one-measure melody. By snapping his fingers and waving his arms to conduct the rhythm while singing the lead melody himself, he led the entire stadium in a collaborative a cappella rendition of “Mayfly“.

  • Even in the subsequent “Kiss Song”, Hua divided fans into three vocal parts to chorus “mo-mo-da-“ (meaning “kiss” in Chinese).

  • Hua created a brand-new intro music for “Ship of Theseus“, and the VJ animation for this intro music incorporated the creative concept of “Luoshan Shadow Puppetry” – an intangible cultural heritage. During the interlude of the performance, Hua performed Tai Chi to the music, a symbol of Chinese culture that originated in Henan Province.

Shenyang

  • On June 28, Hua Chenyu held a concert in Shenyang, Liaoning Province, marking his first concert in China’s northeastern region. He admitted that coming to the Northeast made him want to tell jokes, even imitating famous sketches from the Spring Festival Gala on site and sharing several corny jokes.

  • This was also the last tour concert of the first half of the year, with the next one resuming on August 29 – the day of China’s Qixi Festival (also known as Chinese Valentine’s Day). This schedule was mainly to avoid the intense summer heat, which Hua referred to as the “summer break.”

  • The evening before the concert, Hua held a surprise livestream called “Martian Summer Break Class Meeting”, recounting memories from each concert of the first half of the year in turn.

  • On the day of the concert, a fan couple got married and invited online all fans to attend, then go to the concert together that night. Learning about this via social media, Hua sang “We Meet When Flowers Fade“ with the audience at the venue as a blessing to the newlyweds. He also gifted them tickets to the next concert, inviting them to spend their first Valentine’s Day (Qixi Festival) as a married couple together.

  • In the previous “weather-based bet” in Zhengzhou, it didn’t rain, so the fans won and Hua had to grant one of fans’ requests. Therefore, based on feedback seen in the live stream comments the night before, Hua accompanied the audience in chorusing “Warm House“. Since it was a last-minute decision, neither Hua nor the band had rehearsed it. There were parts where everyone sang wrong, and Hua even made mistakes while playing the piano.

Suzhou

  • From August 29 to 31, Hua Chenyu kicked off the second half of his live tour in Suzhou. Since August 29 (Friday) is “Qixi Festival”, which is the Chinese Valentine’s Day based on the romantic legend of the Zhinü and Niulang, Hua specially arranged a concert on this non-weekend day.

  • At the concert venue, the fences inside the stadium were elaborately decorated with 80,000 red roses, creating a dreamlike “Mars Rose Garden”. On the evening before Qixi Festival, Hua Chenyu specially held a live stream to interact with fans – he not only showed everyone the sea of roses at the venue but also took fans on a first-person view experience of the rehearsal for his aerial entrance during the opening song.

  • This year, the Qixi Festival gift Hua prepared for the on-site audience was a limited-edition puzzle featuring the Mars flower bouquet pattern. Additionally, at the pop-up store, the annual Qixi Festival-limited red butterfly pastries (butterfly-shaped puff pastries) were launched.

  • Hua Chenyu’s connection with Suzhou city dates back to several years ago, when he participated in the singing competition TV show “The Next (天籁之战)“ in Suzhou and commuted there every week. On this show, Hua fully demonstrated his songwriting ability and singing ability, and rearranged many songs that became massive hits.

  • Once again, during the stadium performance, several sophisticated rocket-shaped fireworks were set off while “Eternal Flame“ was being performed.

  • In the previous “weather-based bet” in Shenyang, Hua won again, and he shared another wish: “Actually, I have a small wish that I’ve kept hidden in my heart for more than ten years. Today, just in time for Qixi Valentine’s Day, I want to share this wish. My wish is that all Martians will be happy now and always in the future.” At the same time, Hua held up a carefully prepared “fan service board” (a board with messages for fans), which read, “Martians, strike a heart pose if you feel happy” and “How deep can love be?” Hua continued, “A few days ago, during a music festival in Qingdao, I was also singing ‘King and Beggar‘. Back then, I saw a fan – a little Martian – holding a board that said, ‘Hua Chenyu, strike a heart pose if you feel happy.’ So I struck a heart pose to her. Today, I want to say to all the Martians here: if you feel happy, can you strike a heart pose for me?” The fans responded one after another by striking heart poses.

  • On the third day, it was drizzling. While singing “Arrival“, lightning flashed in the sky, which perfectly matched the stage’s light cascade, creating a unique sense of destiny. During the intro of the most thrilling song “Madhouse“, a huge bolt of lightning also lit up the sky.

  • Hua Chenyu showed off his muscles on stage for the first time. Later, while changing clothes backstage, a colleague teased him, saying, “Bro, that was your first time showing off your muscles on stage, and it looked a bit clumsy.” This made him feel “a little shy – I originally wanted to look cool, but it turned out kind of clumsy.”

Nanjing

  • From October 4th to 6th, during China’s National Day and Mid-Autumn Festival long holiday, the rock theme park-style concerts were held for three consecutive days again after a major upgrade. Additionally, during the holidays before and after the concert (October 1st-3rd, October 7th-8th), the amusement area was open to all the public for free under the name of “Mars Utopia (火星乌托邦)”.

  • On both sides and the back of the “Planet” stage, dozens-of-meters-long elevating extended stages were newly installed.

  • As the stage design for “Ship of Theseus“, a huge inflatable wooden boat was newly placed as a real set in the center of the main stage. This inflatable boat was quickly set up and dismantled by the staff before and after the song performances.

  • Based on previous years’ climate, temperatures drop after autumn in October, so measures like ginger tea were prepared. However, this autumn encountered an unusual heatwave; Hua Chenyu suffered from heatstroke due to the sun during the matinee and thus weared an ice vest again to cool himself down. Moreover, a large number of cooling pads were purchased and provided to the audience for free.




  • The publicly open “Mars Utopia” included more games, food and beverage options, and art installations than the previous park-style concerts.









  • During the three-day concerts, a drone-coordinated fireworks show was held every day, matching the songs “Eternal Flame“, “Bullfight“, “Warm House“, “Light Up the Bonfire at the Galaxy’s End“, as well as the final songs of the matinee and the evening Show, with a different design each day. Hua Chenyu himself stated that the cost of the three-day fireworks show exceeded the value of a villa.

Haikou

  • From November 28 to 30, 2025, the Mars Concert returned to Haikou Changying Wonderland, marking a comeback to the venue of its first Rock Theme Park-Style Concert held in 2021.

  • The concert was originally scheduled to take place near Wenchang Spacecraft Launch Site in Wenchang City, Hainan Province. The aim was to allow the audience to witness the satellite launch by the modified Long March-7 carrier rocket on the evening of November 30 directly from the concert venue. However, due to various reasons, the ongoing stage construction was halted, and the concert had to change venues less than two weeks before the show. It was finally decided to be held at Haikou Changying Wonderland, which had experience hosting Rock Theme Park-Style Concert. The promotional poster also incorporates the elements of the 2021 Mars Concert poster.

  • Prior to the venue change, Haikou Changying Wonderland, the host venue, took the initiative to promise that all audiences could enter the park for free and enjoy all amusement facilities throughout December by presenting their 2021 or 2025 concert tickets.


  • The giant metal ring devices that resemble planetary orbits and surround the entire VIP area, a stage set that had accompanied the Mars Concert for a full three years, was retired after this Haikou concerts. A brand-new set will be used for the Rock Theme Park-Style Concert next year. The ring is affectionately nicknamed “环环 (Huanhuan, which means rings)” by fans, and Zuo Li specially presented Hua Chenyu with a custom plaque commemorating Huanhuan’s retirement during the Matinee on the third day.

  • Inspired by a drum beat-matching game on TikTok, Hua Chenyu set up a drum beat-matching game themed after his song “Bizarre Psychology AKA Quirky Psychology“ in the amusement park. Unfortunately, both he and his band members all failed miserably at the game. He even tried to match the beat during his Evening Show, but failed two nights in a row; instead, the action of holding his head and squatting down after failing actually hit the beat.

  • During the Evening Show on the third day, Hua Chenyu held a live streaming device and broadcast the first-person view of his aerial entrance on the large screen. This fulfilled the promise he had made to fans during a live stream before this year’s Suzhou concerts.

  • Fireworks and drone shows were still featured in Haikou concerts. The drone show on the first day was themed around Mars Concert and roses, while the second day’s theme was Hua’s beloved cat Perper. On the third day, due to the drizzling rain that Hua jokingly called “moisturizing spray”, the drones were obscured by mist and hard for the audience to see. When leaving the stage, Hua Chenyu live-streamed on the large screen and showed the originally planned drone show renderings to the audience with a phone.

  • During the Evening shows on the second and third days, Hua Chenyu took selfies with the audience’s phones at the end of the extended stage.

  • Before the Matinee on the third day, Hua Chenyu went on stage early and took a full lap of selfies with a selfie stick. He later joked that this was to test the temperature and wind speed.

  • At the end of Haikou concerts, Hua Chenyu officially announced that the 2.0 version of the Rock Theme Park-Style Concert would be launched next year, but the specific method needed to be kept confidential to prevent the idea from being plagiarized. He also shared the origin story of the 1.0 version of the concerts that started in 2021. He hopes to be regarded as just an ordinary Martian, and to eat, play, and enjoy ordinary joys with everyone in the Mars Utopia.

Nanning

  • The Mars Concert Tour’s annual cycle is calculated according to the Chinese lunar calendar. Therefore, the Nanning concert on January 2, 2026, still falls under the 2025 tour schedule. The Shenzhen concerts from February 6 to 8 will be the final concerts of Mars Concert 2025.

  • As a New Year’s gift, Hua Chenyu presented every audience with a cloth-made calendar themed around the “Charcoal ball (黑煤球)“.













  • Hua Chenyu announced that the stage art design for this four-sided stage, which has been in use for three years, will come to an end this year. Next year, brand-new stage art design will be adopted not only for the Rock Theme Park-Style Concert, but also for the four-sided stages.

Shenzhen

  • From February 6–8, 2026, Hua Chenyu held the finale of his 2025 concert tour in Shenzhen. Among these dates, February 7th was his 36th birthday, marking his first-ever public birthday celebration since his debut.

  • During the birthday concert on February 7th, fan-organized celebrations included: a collective sing-along of the “Happy Birthday” song following the “3-2-1” countdown of the “Kiss Song”; as Hua walked onto the extended stage, audience members in that section raised different colored fluorescent boards to form massive mosaic characters, while all spectators held up their own cake-shaped ornaments.



  • The concert organizers arranged several tributes, including: distributing confetti popper to every audience member, featuring customized patterns and confetti shapes; a surprise appearance by a mascot in “Charcoal ball (黑煤球)” costume to present a birthday cake after the song “For Forever“, leading the fans in a synchronized birthday chant via the giant screens; and lighting up countless golden LED strips specially draped along the edges of the stadium stands.


  • While performing the song “Conversations with Martian Children“, Hua finally couldn’t help but burst into tears, marking the first time he was too choked up to continue singing; the audience spontaneously took over, singing the entire second half of the song in a loud, powerful chorus.



  • Throughout these three concerts, Hua several times stopped singing during unannounced segments and handed the mic to the audience; every time, the fans caught the lyrics and melody perfectly, leaving him both shocked and joyful.

  • As this was the final stop of the 2025 tour, Hua announced that he had acquired three plots of land in Yunnan, which will be used to build the physical “Mars Home.”
