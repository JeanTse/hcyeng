1. Achievements

  • “Mars Park-Style Concerts 2.0” continues the signature format of Matinee + Evening Show, providing a moment of non-stop revelry for fans to play all day long. Inside the amusement park, a diverse range of entertainment is offered, including integrates gaming, interactive zones, gourmet food, and various leisure activities. The stage design centers on the theme of “Eternity,” shattering the traditional single-format concert model to achieve breakthroughs in both visual presentation and content arrangement. While preserving the iconic artistic style of the Mars Concerts, it deeply integrates natural landscapes to create an exclusive stage that blends high-tech aesthetics with natural vibes, making the audience feel as if they are in a secret “Martian” realm.

  • A brand-new “Twin Setlist” is introduced. By alternately performing different setlists across the tour dates, each show is given distinct highlights. Through this multi-layered musical experience, the audience can fully appreciate the unique charm of the stage created by Hua Chenyu.

2. Anecdote

Yuxi (Cancelled)

  • The performances scheduled for May 1-2 and 4-5, 2026, which were to serve as the opening stop for “Mars Park-Style Concerts 2.0” at Fuxian Lake (the second deepest freshwater lake in China) in Yuxi, Yunnan, were abruptly cancelled due to force majeure on April 22 – just one week before the event. Dismantling of the stage facilities, which cost over 2 billion JPY and were nearly complete, began that very night.

  • According to media interviews and reports published before the cancellation, the decision to locate “Mars Park-Style Concerts 2.0” at Fuxian Lake was prompted by a warm invitation from Yuxi City. The event’s realization came only after navigating numerous challenges and setbacks.

  • According to various estimates, the losses incurred by Hua Chenyu and the organizers from stage construction costs, hundreds of thousands of ticket refunds, VIP ticket perks and merchandise for sale, and compensation for flight and hotel cancellations alone will reach billions of JPY. This does not include investments in fixed assets such as local real estate purchases, infrastructure development, environmental protection support, and the establishment of supporting facilities.

  • At the final concert last year, Hua Chenyu announced the purchase of three plots of land in Yunnan to build a physical “Mars Home”. These plots were designated for a permanent stage and audience area, lifestyle facilities like B&Bs and cafes, and the creation of a “Mars Utopia”. When the concert was cancelled, the B&B and cafe were already in operation. In particular, the B&B “Warm House” had previously selected guests (limited to concert-goers) via livestream to stay during the concert period for a special price of 3 RMB per day; all payments have now been refunded, and the offer has been changed to allow all booked guests to stay for free.

  • On the night the cancellation was announced, Hua Chenyu held a Livestream, performing several songs rearranged specifically for the concerts to comfort his fans. A few days later, at the China Blue Zuili Lake Music Festival, Hua Chenyu revealed that on the very night he learned of the cancellation, he had organized a specialized task force to scout other Chinese cities for a new venue and had already dispatched them to the field.
