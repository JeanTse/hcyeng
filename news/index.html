火星演唱会 MARS CONCERT 2026 - Yuxi Concert Cancelled

The performances scheduled for May 1-2 and 4-5, 2026, which were to serve as the opening stop for “Mars Park-Style Concerts 2.0” at Fuxian Lake (the second deepest freshwater lake in China) in Yuxi, Yunnan, were abruptly cancelled due to force majeure on April 22 – just one week before the event. Dismantling of the stage facilities, which cost over 2 billion JPY and were nearly complete, began that very night.
According to media interviews and reports published before the cancellation, the decision to locate “Mars Park-Style Concerts 2.0” at Fuxian Lake was prompted by a warm invitation from Yuxi City. The event’s realization came only after navigating numerous challenges and setbacks.
According to various estimates, the losses incurred by Hua Chenyu and the organizers from stage construction costs, hundreds of thousands of ticket refunds, VIP ticket perks and merchandise for sale, and compensation for flight and hotel cancellations alone will reach billions of JPY. This does not include investments in fixed assets such as local real estate purchases, infrastructure development, environmental protection support, and the establishment of supporting facilities.
On the night the cancellation was announced, Hua Chenyu held a Livestream, performing several songs rearranged specifically for the concerts to comfort his fans. A few days later, at the China Blue Zuili Lake Music Festival, Hua Chenyu revealed that on the very night he learned of the cancellation, he had organized a specialized task force to scout other Chinese cities for a new venue and had already dispatched them to the field.

火星演唱会 MARS CONCERT 2025 - Haikou Concerts

From November 28 to 30, 2025, the Mars Concert returned to Haikou Changying Wonderland, marking a comeback to the venue of its first Rock Theme Park-Style Concert held in 2021.
The concert was originally scheduled to take place near Wenchang Spacecraft Launch Site in Wenchang City, Hainan Province. The aim was to allow the audience to witness the satellite launch by the modified Long March-7 carrier rocket on the evening of November 30 directly from the concert venue. However, due to various reasons, the ongoing stage construction was halted, and the concert had to change venues less than two weeks before the show. It was finally decided to be held at Haikou Changying Wonderland, which had experience hosting Rock Theme Park-Style Concert.
The giant metal ring devices that resemble planetary orbits and surround the entire VIP area, a stage set that had accompanied the Mars Concert for a full three years, was retired after this Haikou concerts. A brand-new set will be used for the Rock Theme Park-Style Concert next year. The ring is affectionately nicknamed “环环 (Huanhuan, which means rings)” by fans, and Zuo Li specially presented Hua Chenyu with a custom plaque commemorating Huanhuan’s retirement during the Matinee on the third day.
At the end of Haikou concerts, Hua Chenyu officially announced that the 2.0 version of the Rock Theme Park-Style Concert would be launched next year, but the specific method needed to be kept confidential to prevent the idea from being plagiarized. He also shared the origin story of the 1.0 version of the concerts that started in 2021. On May 13, 2025, the MV of the song “Bizarre Psychology AKA Quirky Psychology “ was officially released.

火星演唱会 MARS CONCERT 2025 - Foshan Concerts

China celebrated Labor Day from May 1 to 5, and Hua Chenyu held three consecutive Theme Park-style Concert from May 2 to 4 in Foshan. The third day (May 4) marked the first anniversary of the sunrise concert, and Hua prepared many surprises for the Martians.
On the first day of the concert, the weather was hot. On the second day, there was a brief heavy rain during the middle of the event. The game segment was skipped, and many pieces of equipment were damaged. Repairs caused a 20-minute delay to the evening session. During the rain, audiences were allowed to leave the venue to take a break, and re-entry was arranged once the rain had stopped.
May 4 is the first anniversary of the sunrise concert. 2,000 drones were launched to simulate the sun and spell out the words: “You are my sun — by Big Brother. (你们就是我的太阳 ——大哥)”, setting a record for the number of drones launched above the concert venue. (Due to the large gathering of people at the performance site, for safety reasons, the launch of such a large number of drones is usually not allowed.)

April

“Never Cross Paths AKA Eternal Farewell (不重逢)“‘s MV

On April 23, 2025, the MV of the song “Never Cross Paths AKA Eternal Farewell (不重逢)“ was officially released.

Beloong Music Festival, Wuhang

After six years, on April 20, Hua Chenyu returned to the music festival stage! With his full concert band lineup and brand-new VJ animations specially created for the festival. Hua delivered six songs to his hometown audience in Wuhan. The setlist included fan-dubbed “commercial performance package” like “I Don’t Care (我管你)“ and “For Forever“, alongside recent releases “Black and White Artist“, “Bizarre Psychology AKA Quirky Psychology“, “Extraordinary Ordinary Life“, and “Never Cross Paths AKA Eternal Farewell “, culminating with the classic Martian love ballad “King and Beggar“.
Notably, “Black and White Artist“ marked its debut performance outside Hua’s solo concerts.

Global celebrity endorsement for ENYA MUSIC

On April 20, 2025, Hua Chenyu officially announced his appointment as the global celebrity endorsement for ENYA MUSIC, alongside assuming the role of Chief Exploration Officer for the CYBER-G Stringless Smart Guitar. Hua also collaborated with ENYA MUSIC to create the Mars Cyber-G Co-branding Gift Box.
ENYA MUSIC is an innovative technology music company, integrating product development, manufacturing, operation and brand building in various music fields, such as musical instruments, smart audio, music culture, and music education.

火星演唱会 MARS CONCERT 2025 - Xi’an Concerts

April 5-6 (Qingming Festival holiday in China), Hua Chenyu held consecutive concerts in Xi’an, an ancient capital of 13 dynasties.
On Day 1, Hua mentioned “keeping the performance more family-friendly” as his father and sister were in the audience, while teasing a “an outfit requiring prolonged mental preparation” for Day 2. Fans speculated wildly about its boldness, only to discover a full-body leopard print ensemble the next day.
The most renowned historical site in Xi’an is the Mausoleum of the First Qin Emperor’s Terracotta Army. Staffs from a Terracotta Warriors-themed musical troupe watched the lives in Qin Shi Huang and warrior cosplay. During the performance of “Light Up the Bonfire at the Galaxy’s End“ they joined fans’ dancing in the arena pit.
While filming a vlog at a snack street the day before, Hua passed by a fan girl without being recognized. At the concert, Hua publicly screened the coincidentally recorded encounter, jokingly awarding her an autographed “reunions feel distant” (from “In the Morning Light, There’s You“ lyrics) certificate, and gifted her a whole set of merchandise.
Having lost the weather-based bet with fans (no rain occurred), Hua voluntarily proposed fulfilling the wager by dancing to choreography from his “Bizarre Psychology AKA Quirky Psychology“ MV - his first official concert dance performance. On Day 2, Hua executed the routine wearing Lop-eared rabbit shaped woolen hat, then turned completely red from shyness.

March

火星演唱会 MARS CONCERT 2025 - Changsha Concerts

On March 22-23, Hua Chenyu returned to his debut city Changsha to hold Concerts for two consecutive days.
A drone conducting high-speed aerial filming around and the stage crashed at a corner of the platform, with the entire incident broadcast live on the giant screen in first-person perspective.
While singing the line “There is a letter, A letter for you from the future” in “Let’s Go and Watch the Sunrise“, Hua pulled out a letter from his chest. The stationery featured a simple sketch widely cherished among the fans: Hua and ET sharing a kiss at sunrise.
During the second day’s concert, Hua made a weather-based bet with fans, rooted in his “Little Sun” legend (believed to bring sunshine to concert dates). Despite prior continuous rain, Changsha experienced unusually scorching weather during the concerts, followed by a sudden temperature drop the next day. Hua pledged that If the next concert in Xi’an also has clear skies, fans win—and he’ll grant one collective wish.

Voice-Over - “Spring in China (春天里的中国)”

On March 18, 2025, Xinhua News Agency released a series of spring scenery videos from across China on Weibo, themed “Spring in China: Vibrant with Vitality, Pulsating with Energy”‌.
Hua Chenyu, as the Special Narrator, provided voice-over commentary for the video series witnessing the “ordinary yet great moments of strivers”‌.
The video, with a duration of 1 minute and 30 seconds, features the instrumental version of Hua’s original song “Beauty Yet Revealed to Me“ as the background music.‌

MARS CONCERT 2025 - Fuzhou Concerts

On March 8-9, Hua Chenyu launched his annual tour with two consecutive concerts in Fuzhou, the capital of Fujian Province in southern China.
This year, Hua Chenyu has completely updated the setlist and arrangements. The arrangement theme is “East-West Fusion,” skillfully blending traditional Chinese instruments such as guzheng, xiao, and pipa with Western instruments like harp, Scottish bagpipes, and Irish flutes. Through the collision of Eastern and Western musical elements and cross-cultural art fusion, he has expanded his musical boundaries anew.

Short Movie “Luck Behind the Door (开门大吉)“

On January 23, 2025, Hua Chenyu played the role of the manager of Peace Grand Hotel in the Spring Festival short film “Luck Behind the Door (开门大吉)“ produced by the mobile game “Game For Peace”.

Releas a New Album “Quantitative Change Tipping Point“

On January 10, 2025, Hua Chenyu released his sixth album, “Quantitative Change Tipping Point (量变临界点)“, which is also a full original album.

In addition, Hua and Idol group TNT (时代少年团) together performed the song “我要你管”, a mix of their respective masterpieces “I Don’t Care (我管你)“ and “要你管”.

November

MARS CONCERT 2024 - Shenzhen

On November 29-30, Hua Chenyu held the final concert of this year’s tour in Shenzhen, Guangdong Province.
The Mars Concert 2019 held in Haikou was originally scheduled to be held in Shenzhen, and tickets were already sold out, but it was cancelled due to force majeure. This year, Hua Chenyu finally fulfilled the promise he made to Shenzhen five years ago.
For the promotional poster, Hua also chose to use the 2019 version.
A new song will be released in each city in the second half of the year. This time, Hua debuted the last new song “In the Morning Light, There’s You (晨光里有你)“. The word “晨光” in the song title means “morning light” and includes the same Chinese character as “晨” in Hua Chenyu’s name, so the song title can also be interpreted as “Hua Chenyu, in the light, there’s you.”
The design of the Rock Theme Park-Style concerts (Guiyang Concert) was transplanted to the stadium, achieving rocket-shaped fireworks and high-pressure fountain mist spraying.
On the night before the concert, Hua held a live broadcast on SNS with the theme of “Mars Year-End Party”. Wearing a black suit, Hua imitated the style of a company boss while summarizing this year’s live tour and sharing many anecdotes related to the concerts.

MARS CONCERT 2024 - Wuhan

On November 10, Hua Chenyu continued his live tour in his hometown of Wuhan.
The opening song was changed to “You Have To Believe This Is Not the Last Day (你要相信这不是最后一天)“, a charity song Hua wrote to support the citizens of Wuhan who have been severely hurt by COVID 19.
Wuhan is known as the “Breakfast Capital” of China because of the variety of breakfast options available, and eating breakfast is called “passing breakfast (过早)” in local dialect. On November 7, Hua rode a shared bike alone early in the morning, had breakfast (hot dry noodles, bean curd skin, eggnog), and broadcast the whole thing live on his mobile phone.
The day after the concert, Hua Chenyu strolled to the popular check-in spot on Mars where a large number of fans gathered - Wuhan Conservatory of Music (Hua’s alma mater), and lined up with fans to buy the dessert that became popular because of his own favorite.

eSports Documentary “One Shot (一竞到底)“

Hua Chenyu acted as the narrator in the movie “One Shot (一竞到底)“, China’s first documentary about e-sports.
The film is based on the e-sports athletes winning the gold medal in the “Game For Peace” events at the 2022 Asian Games, focusing on the on-field stories of three well-known e-sports athletes, showcasing the difficulties and tenacious fighting spirit on the way to pursue their dreams, as well as the e-sports spirit shared by different e-sports players and e-sports practitioners.
Director: Yang Fan
Screening time: November 1, 2024

October

MARS CONCERT 2024 - Hangzhou

On August 24, Hua Chenyu continued his live tour in Hangzhou. Hangzhou’s newly built “Big Lotus” Stadium is second only to Beijing’s Bird’s Nest in terms of area. The audience for this concert exceeded 80,000 people.
A new song will be released in each city in the second half of the year. This time, Hua debuted his new song “Human’s Love (人之爱)“.
Taking the stage with a long-lost short hairstyle
On the evening before the concert, Hua live-streamed himself touring the stadium. It was the first time for him to personally watch the stage effects from the various levels of the stands, and he said it was much more beautiful and spectacular than what he could see on the stage.
During the concert, Hua recalled the Hangzhou stop of the “Superboy 2013“ live tour which he took part in shortly after his debut, and impromptu sang a few lines of “Mr. Almost (差不多先生)”, which he sang back then.

Participated in the PEACE NIGHT of “Game For Peace” as a celebrity endorsement

All four celebrity endorsements of “Game For Peace” (Hua Chenyu, Victoria Song, Tian Xiwei, Esther Yu) appeared on the red carpet in cosplay. Hua cosplayed the new character “space-time Concerto: Spark (时空协奏曲：火花)”.
Hua sang three theme songs: “Spark·Flame (火花Flame)“, “Iconic moment“, “Flying Conductor (飞行指挥家)“.
In the exhibition match, Hua teamed up with three other celebrity endorsements to win, scoring 11 kills solo.

Character Song “Spark·Flame (火花Flame)“

On October 13, 2024, character song “Spark·Flame (火花Flame)“ for the Peace Super Group “space-time Concerto: Spark (时空协奏曲：火花)”, sung by Hua Chenyu, was officially released.
Hua cosplayed as this character to film the MV.

MARS CONCERT 2024 - Guiyang

On October 4-6, Hua Chenyu held his last rock festival-style concert of this year for three consecutive days in Guiyang City, Guizhou Province, southwest China.
Fireworks were allowed at the venue, and not only were fireworks set off during the last song, but also during the song “Eternal Flame“, several sophisticated rocket-shaped fireworks were set off.
At the Matinee on the third day, Hua impromptu pulled his friend Ning Huanyu, whom he met on his debut show “Superboy 2013“, from the audience to the stage, and together with Zuo Li, the three of them sang “Follow Me“, a collaborative song composed by Ning.

Magazine“Madame Figaro HOMMES” Issue Oct 2024

The magazine has designed 2 covers for selection. Comes with 5 photocards.
There is also a “Mars Concert Special Issue” (C set), which includes one poster and 5 stickers of stage costume.

September

MARS CONCERT 2024 - Beijing

On September 7-8, Hua Chenyu performed at the National Stadium (Bird’s Nest) for the third time.
September 8 marks the sixth anniversary of Hua’s first personal concert at the Bird’s Nest. As a commemoration, Hua Chenyu sent physical gifts by courier to all the audience members who purchased tickets through official channels. The gifts include perfumes, scented candles, sachets, hand creams and a variety of T-shirts. The gifts were designed by artist Song Santu and were also exhibited as part of an installation art piece at Mars Concert 2023.
At the end of the set list, “Growing Toward the Sun - Sunrise Ver.“ was sung, recreating the grand occasion of the Sunrise Concert.
A new song will be released in each city in the second half of the year. This time, Hua debuted his new song Never Cross Paths AKA Eternal Farewell (不重逢)“.

Celebrity Endorsement for Penfolds

On September 4, 2024, Hua Chenyu served as the celebrity endorsement for Australia’s Penfolds, one of the world’s top wineries.

Won the Platinum Award at the MUSE Design Awards

The stage design of Mars Concert has won the Platinum Award at the MUSE Design Awards three times: 2021’s Rock Theme Park-Style Concerts, 2023’s four-sided stage in the Bird’s Nest, 2024’s Sunrise Concert.
